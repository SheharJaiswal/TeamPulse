# Open Questions
Resolve through SDD before implementing related behavior.

1. Is employee onboarding exclusively HR invitation/provisioning, or can organizations enable self-registration?
2. What is the authoritative source of organization/team membership?
3. Which challenge scoring models are required: raw totals, averages, normalized completion or participation rate?
4. What minimum cohort size prevents re-identification in team insights?
5. Which activities require evidence/verification versus employee self-report?
6. Can one personal habit link to multiple programmes, and how are target mismatches handled?
7. Are manager permissions limited to direct reports or configurable team scopes?
8. What retention/deletion policy applies to activity and audit data?
9. What employee account lifecycle events can deactivate or reactivate an account, which authority may perform those actions, and what audit/reason requirements apply?
